How these are made
  • One strict look: paper collage, torn edges, pastels. Wobble reads as craft.
  • A paper character with a small wish, and one question written on a prop in the scene.
  • Shared motifs: lantern = activation, paper plane = information moving, mind egg = the model, chalk arrow = gradient.
  • Honest motion: every moving number is real maths (softmax, gradients, dot products), computed live.
  • A "where this story stops being true" beat, then objects morph into the real ARENA code.
  • Ends on a steering line and a "now you drive" coda you can drag and tap.
